# Ringdown privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Ringdown is an Android live wallpaper app made by Nandyala Sai Ganesh Poshith. This policy explains what
happens to your information when you use it. Ringdown is open source, so you can check everything below in
the code: <https://github.com/poshithNandyala/ringdown>

## What stays on your phone

Your habit name, the number of days, your start date and your look settings (style, colors, size) are saved
only on your phone. Ringdown has no account and no sign-in, and it never sends these settings anywhere.

If you use Android's backup, Android may include these settings in your device backup to your own Google
account, like other apps' settings. You can turn this off in Android's settings.

## What Ringdown doesn't do

- It doesn't collect, sell or share personal information.
- It has no analytics or tracking.
- It doesn't use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

## Update and news checks

A few times a day, Ringdown downloads a small public settings file from GitHub
(`raw.githubusercontent.com`), or from the jsDelivr mirror (`cdn.jsdelivr.net`) if GitHub can't be reached.
The file tells the app whether a new version is out, and lists any news or extra pages. Ringdown sends nothing
about you in this request. Like any website, these services receive basic connection information such as
your IP address; their own privacy policies apply ([GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement),
[jsDelivr](https://jsdelivr.com/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net)). I don't receive or store this information.

## Extra pages

If you open a page from the app's "More" section, the app loads that web page like a browser would. The
website sees basic connection information, and its own privacy policy applies.

## Permissions

- **Internet:** to download the settings file and show extra pages.
- **Notifications:** to tell you about new versions and news about Ringdown. You can turn them off in
  Android's settings at any time.
- **Vibrate:** for the optional small buzz when a day completes.

## Children

Ringdown is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

## Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at <https://github.com/poshithNandyala/ringdown/issues>.
