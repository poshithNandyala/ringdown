## Full description (4000 characters max)

```text
Ringdown turns your habit into a live wallpaper you see every time you pick up your phone.

BREAK A HABIT
Pick what you're quitting (sugar, smoking, social media, anything) and for how many days. All the rings start lit. Every day you stay away from it, one ring lifts off, breaks apart in a small burst of light, and the count goes down. When the last ring breaks, you've done it.

BUILD A HABIT
Pick what you want to do every day: gym, reading, coding practice, meditation, anything. The rings start as a faint outline. Every day you show up, one ring gets built, until the whole shape is complete.

WHY RINGS?
Habits run as loops in your brain: a cue, a routine, a reward. Each ring stands for one day of that loop getting weaker, or stronger. The built-in guide explains the research behind it, with sources.

FEATURES
• Break or build mode
• 32 ready-made habits, or name your own
• Any length from 1 to 365 days. Already on a streak? Enter the days you've done
• Three 3D styles: Orb, Grid and Tunnel
• Six color themes, plus size and position controls
• A small celebration every day, and bigger ones at milestones
• Tap the wallpaper to make the rings react; double-tap to replay today's animation
• Works on your home screen and lock screen
• Battery saver mode
• No account, no sign-up, no tracking
• Free and open source

Ringdown is a motivation tool, not medical treatment. If you're stopping alcohol, drugs or a medicine, talk to a doctor first.
```
